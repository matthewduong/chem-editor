| `<graphic GraphicType="Line">` does not round-trip through real ChemDraw                  | **investigated 2026-10-12, not fixable by changing what we write**                     | ChemDraw converts a Line graphic to a `SupersededBy`-marked stub plus a new `<arrow>` on every resave. Three things were fed to it to find a stable form: our `<graphic GraphicType="Line">`, a raw headless `<arrow ArrowheadHead="None" ArrowheadTail="None">`, and ChemDraw's own exact graphic+arrow output handed straight back in. **All three get rewritten again, with a new arrow id each time** — this is ChemDraw's own save behaviour being non-idempotent for this construct, not a property of what we export. `GraphicType="Oval"` round-trips fine by contrast. Left as `<graphic GraphicType="Line">`, which is DTD-valid and renders correctly on open; switching to an arrow-shaped export was tried and produces no improvement. The one real, fixable gap this surfaced — our importer not skipping a `SupersededBy`-marked `<arrow>`, which would otherwise import as a live duplicate alongside its replacement — is fixed, matching the generic-graphic importer's existing handling; regression test in `tests/cdxml-round-trip-regressions.test.mjs`. |
